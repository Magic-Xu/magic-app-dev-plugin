# Indie App Demand Research

Help the user decide which problem is worth validating and what evidence would justify building. Prioritize paid
demand, reachable users, and a feasible operating model under the user's constraints.

## Match The Requested Decision

- For opportunity discovery, compare credible directions and recommend what to validate next.
- For a named idea, investigate its audience, painful job, willingness to pay, reachability, and decisive unknowns.
- For a supplied article or founder thread, extract supported demand lessons and their limits. Expand into candidate
  research or a validation plan only when requested or needed to answer the user's decision.

Reuse known platform, strengths, budget, time horizon, geography, operating-cost tolerance, and category constraints.
Ask when a missing constraint materially changes the recommendation; continue research that is independent of it.
Local-first and low ongoing cost are defaults, subject to the user's actual product and monetization choices.

## Gather Decision-Relevant Evidence

Browse for current revenue, pricing, rankings, competitors, platform capabilities, and founder claims. Prefer
primary sources and record retrieval dates for facts likely to drift.

- For X/Twitter links, fetch the original when possible. If blocked, a public mirror or syndication endpoint may
  recover the content; identify the source and verification limits. Respect any user restriction on sources.
- For TrustMRR, prefer its documented public AI endpoint when available and respect site access rules.
- Add store reviews, search demand, community complaints, substitutes, or acquisition evidence when they can change
  the decision. Distinguish verified data, self-reported claims, and inference.

Paid use, repeated specific problems, costly substitutes, and manual workarounds can support demand. Likes, views,
and downloads alone do not establish payment intent or retention. Separate a reachable acquisition channel from
proof that users value the product. Assess frequency and urgency in context: an infrequent high-consequence job
can still support a useful paid product.

## Compare Feasibility And Tradeoffs

Evaluate demand confidence, willingness to pay, distribution, differentiation, build effort, ongoing cost, and
material platform or policy dependencies. Under a local-first constraint, favor on-device core value and assess
whether privacy or offline use matters to buyers. Account for inference, storage, moderation, support, and other
operating costs where they actually arise.

Use a qualitative comparison or a scored matrix only when it makes the tradeoffs clearer. If scoring helps,
explain the dimensions and priorities; keep evidence confidence separate from attractiveness and mark unknowns.
A total score is a comparison aid, not proof of demand. Do not rank a poorly evidenced idea highly just to fill a list.

## Choose The Next Validation

Choose the least costly test that can resolve the decisive uncertainty. Possible tests include targeted interviews,
store/search analysis, a priced landing page, a manual service, a prototype, or a tiny paid utility. Select according
to whether the unknown concerns pain, payment, distribution, feasibility, or repeat use; there is no fixed order.

Define the hypothesis, audience/channel, observable outcome, time or budget bound, and decision criterion. Calibrate
thresholds to the price, channel, sample, expected economics, and cost of being wrong. Label provisional thresholds
as hypotheses and retain uncertainty for small samples; do not present universal click or interview quotas as proof.
Proposing a validation experiment does not authorize publishing it, taking payment, or contacting people.

## Deliver The Decision

Use the form that answers the request. An opportunity shortlist should identify the target user, painful job,
supporting evidence, first complete product loop, monetization, reachable channel, key risk, and next validation.
A source summary or focused idea review can be shorter. Lead with the supported recommendation or conclusion,
separate facts from assumptions, and explain evidence gaps that could change it.

## Handoff To Product Planning

When the user chooses an opportunity, carry the evidence, target user/job, unresolved demand and
distribution risks into [产品规划](../local-first-android-app-builder/SKILL.md). Retain the original
validation criterion for [later outcome checks](../app-end-to-end-delivery/references/iteration-outcomes.md).
A research request alone does not authorize creating a product, account or repository.
